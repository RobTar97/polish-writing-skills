# Domain safeguards

Apply the common transformation contract in every domain, then protect the domain-specific elements below.

## Administrative content

Lead with status, action, deadline, delivery method, and consequence before institutional background when the source permits it.

Preserve exactly:

- legal basis and formal procedural term;
- who must act and who has authority;
- whether an action is optional, permitted, or required;
- how a deadline is calculated: issue, receipt, delivery, event, calendar or working days;
- required attachments, signature, form, channel, and destination;
- consequence of no action and routes of appeal or review.

Do not turn `odwołanie` into a generic request or `30 dni` into `miesiąc`.

## Financial content

Preserve amounts, currencies, percentages, rates, fees, calculation bases, periods, payment dates, thresholds, examples, guarantees, exclusions, and late-payment consequences.

Keep distinctions such as:

- illustrative versus guaranteed;
- gross versus net;
- fixed versus variable;
- nominal versus effective rate;
- fee versus interest;
- calendar versus working day.

Never approximate a precise figure unless the source authorizes approximation.

## Healthcare content

Preserve medicine and active-ingredient names, dose, unit, route, frequency, duration, timing, age and weight thresholds, contraindications, symptoms, test values, urgency, exceptions, uncertainty, and instructions to contact a professional.

Use layered explanations when safe: retain the clinical term, then explain it plainly. Do not broaden or weaken `nie stosować`, invent a clinical threshold, convert units, or replace professional advice with an agent's recommendation.

## Instructional content

Write observable actions in execution order. State prerequisites before controlled steps and expected results afterward. Preserve literal interface labels, commands, filenames, paths, values, and branching logic.

Do not add a button, recovery step, or product behavior that the source does not establish.

## Customer-service and interface content

Answer, when known:

1. What happened?
2. What does it mean for the reader?
3. What can the reader do now?
4. What happens next?

Preserve transaction state, responsibility, charges, delivery status, account state, support route, time estimate, and retry consequences. If a system state is unknown, say what can be checked instead of inventing reassurance.

For locale resources, preserve keys, placeholders, tags, escape sequences, ICU branches, and runtime syntax. Plain-language review is not a substitute for product-localization QA.

## Legal and regulated content

Plain language may reorder and explain legal communication, but it must preserve the mechanism through which readers exercise rights and obligations. Flag specialist review when interpretation, legal sufficiency, regulatory policy, or jurisdiction-specific drafting is material.
