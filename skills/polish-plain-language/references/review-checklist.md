# Review checklist

1. Confirm the intended reader, domain, purpose, relationship, channel, register, format, and requested adaptation depth.
2. Confirm the reader's task or information need and whether the output exposes it early enough.
3. Compare source and output for names, numbers, dates, periods, amounts, percentages, units, references, UI labels, URLs, quotations, terminology, negation, modality, conditions, exceptions, causality, chronology, rights, obligations, responsibility, and warning strength.
4. Confirm that no cause, reassurance, deadline, remedy, eligibility rule, consequence, contact route, recovery step, or procedural right was invented.
5. Check that status, action, prerequisites, deadline, consequence, exceptions, explanation, and help appear in a reader-appropriate order.
6. Check that combined conditions remain combined and visibly connected to their actions or consequences.
7. Check stable terminology, necessary definitions, first-use abbreviation expansion, precise references, and literal UI labels.
8. Check sentence structure, clause depth, nominalizations, institutional framing, word order, actor visibility, agreement, government, punctuation, and typography.
9. Confirm that headings predict useful content and that lists represent real sequences, requirements, options, comparisons, or parallel items.
10. Confirm that instructions state observable actions in execution order and that warnings expose risk, condition, consequence, and safe action when known.
11. Confirm that the output does not claim ETR validation, digital-accessibility compliance, legal sufficiency, medical safety, or financial compliance from language editing alone.
12. Test whether the reader can quickly answer: What happened? Does this apply to me? What must or may I do? By when? How? What happens next? What exceptions, risks, or help routes matter?
13. Return the finished Polish artifact first and add only material notes unless the user requests an audit.
