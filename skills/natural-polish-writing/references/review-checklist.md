# Review checklist

1. Confirm the purpose, audience, genre, channel, register, format, and requested edit depth.
2. Compare the result with the semantic lock: facts, numbers, units, dates, names, attribution, quotations, citations, URLs, negation, modality, conditions, causality, chronology, terminology, commitments, and voice.
3. Confirm that no example, mechanism, opinion, experience, apology, promise, deadline, permission, or evidence was invented.
4. For translations, confirm that every material proposition and qualification survived and that protected source elements remain intact.
5. Check information order, paragraph purpose, repetition, connective density, terminology stability, collocations, and register fit.
6. Check syntax, agreement, government, case, aspect, tense, reflexive possession, antecedents, word order, and sentence boundaries.
7. Check spelling and punctuation against RJP 2026; check quotation marks, dash function, capitalization, and spacing against the document's house style.
8. Confirm that deliberate voice, humor, informality, technical density, academic caution, or marked emphasis was not normalized away.
9. For structured UI or sensitive formal correspondence, confirm that the safe-pass boundary was respected and state any required specialist follow-up briefly.
10. Return the polished Polish artifact first. Add only material notes in the language of the user's request unless a detailed audit was requested.
