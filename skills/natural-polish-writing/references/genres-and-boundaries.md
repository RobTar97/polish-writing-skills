# Genres and boundaries

Select a profile from the request and the source. Do not flatten every genre into casual plain Polish.

| Work | Starting approach | Preserve or avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article or explanatory prose | Put useful information early and connect claim, evidence, and qualification | Preserve the author's argument and intended level of detail; avoid empty scene-setting |
| Marketing or web prose | Prefer a specific supported benefit, mechanism, or action | Preserve approved claims and product names; avoid invented proof, superlatives, and fake urgency |
| Ordinary message or notice | Make purpose, action, timing, and responsibility easy to find | Adjust courtesy without adding apologies, promises, deadlines, or authority |
| Social post | Match the account's voice, compression, line breaks, and degree of informality | Preserve deliberate slang, emojis, and irregular rhythm; do not introduce fake mistakes or persona |
| Academic or technical prose | Preserve terminology, method, scope, cautious modality, and field convention | Do not force conversationality, active voice, short sentences, or common-language substitutes |
| Translation into Polish | Translate meaning faithfully, then edit for Polish relationships and register | Preserve source uncertainty, attribution, structure, and protected wording |

## Handle mixed or missing context

Infer the dominant profile from purpose, vocabulary, structure, and channel. When a text mixes profiles, apply decisions locally—for example, keep an academic quotation formal inside an accessible article. Ask a question only if competing interpretations would materially change meaning, relationship, or format.

Use neutral contemporary Polish as a fallback, not as permission to erase a recognizable voice.

## Respect scope boundaries

For UI strings, locale files, keys, placeholders, tags, ICU branches, or other structured resources:

1. Preserve every technical token and runtime branch exactly.
2. Improve only language that can be changed safely from the available context.
3. Avoid inventing product behavior, recovery steps, limits, or button labels not supported by the source.
4. Note briefly that interaction design and localization QA require a specialized product-localization pass.

For relationship-sensitive formal correspondence, regulated statements, executive communication, press-release strategy, or organizational protocol:

1. Preserve roles, commitments, approvals, deadlines, legal force, and the requested level of courtesy.
2. Improve clarity and naturalness without designing missing protocol or claiming authority.
3. Note briefly when specialized business-writing, legal, or domain review remains necessary.

It is acceptable to polish ordinary professional prose fully when relationship strategy, resource integrity, or regulated meaning is not the central task.
